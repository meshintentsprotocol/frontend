> **The Next Generation of Liquidity & Intent Execution on Bitcoin Cash**

> *A non-custodial, active market-making and shared liquidity protocol engineered with native CashScript covenants, CashTokens, and zero-inventory solver execution.*

## 1. Executive Summary

Liquidity on decentralized exchanges has long suffered from rigid capital locking, impermanent loss, and socialized fee dilution. On Bitcoin Cash (BCH), developers historically faced an added dilemma: how to create dynamic, highly capital-efficient market-making on a UTXO chain where balances cannot be passively referenced across multiple concurrent transactions.

**BCHMesh** is a modular liquidity and intent settlement protocol built directly on Bitcoin Cash using native **P2SH32 covenants** and **CashTokens** (`CHIP-2021-02`). 

Instead of forcing Liquidity Providers (LPs) to deposit paired tokens into monolithic AMM pools, BCHMesh empowers users to deploy **isolated, active strategy covenants** that:
* Capture **100% of trading fees** exclusively for the deployer.
* **Auto-recycle capital** into reverse buy orders when filled.
* Enable **atomic bulk rebalancing** of dozens of positions in **one transaction for ~$0.01 USD**.
* Power **zero-inventory solvers** that seamlessly match trader intents against active on-chain strategies.

## 2. The Problem with Traditional AMM DEXs

Traditional Automated Market Makers (such as Uniswap v2 and Cauldron on BCH) established decentralized trading, but they carry severe structural liabilities:

### 1. Socialized Fee Dilution
When you deposit capital into a pooled AMM, you receive generic LP pool tokens. Trading fees generated by high-volume swings are split proportionally among all pool participants—including passive, stale capital and just-in-time (JIT) fee snipers.

### 2. Capital Inefficiency & Idle Funds
In standard constant-product AMMs ($x \cdot y = k$), capital is distributed across the entire infinite price curve ($0 \to \infty$). Over 90% of your deposited funds sit idle, failing to earn yield while still being fully exposed to impermanent loss.

### 3. Rigid 50/50 Dual-Asset Requirements
Depositing requires holding both sides of a pair (e.g., 50% BCH and 50% pUSD). If you only hold BCH, you must manually sell half your stack, incurring taxable events, slippage, and swap fees before you can even begin earning.

### 4. High Gas Friction for Rebalancing (EVM vs. UTXO)
On EVM chains, adjusting multiple limit orders or concentrated ranges requires sequential contract invocations, easily costing $15 to $80 per rebalance. This prices individual LPs out of active market making.

## 4. How BCHMesh Extends Liquidity Usage

BCHMesh fundamentally expands how liquidity operates on Bitcoin Cash:

### A. Single-Asset, Multi-Market Deployment (100% Shared Liquidity Across All Routes)
With BCHMesh, you do not need paired inventory, nor do you fragment your capital across isolated pools. A market maker holding pure BCH can call `ship()` in a single transaction to deploy liquidity across multiple trading pairs simultaneously, where **100% of the single-asset inventory is concurrently available to satisfy all configured routes**:
* **100% available to `BCH -> pUSD`** (Stablecoin pair)
* **100% available to `BCH -> mUSD`** (Algorithmic stable pair)
* **100% available to `BCH -> FUR`** (Ecosystem token pair)

Instead of fragmenting a 10 BCH balance into $4\text{ BCH} / 3\text{ BCH} / 3\text{ BCH}$, the entire 10 BCH backs all three routes at once—generating **3x virtual liquidity depth (30 BCH equivalent market depth)**. Whichever route gets filled first consumes the inventory (or partial fills atomically adjust the remaining balance), and the incoming payment tokens automatically recycle into the appropriate counter-strategy!

### B. Closed-Loop Capital Recycling
When a taker buys BCH from your `BCH -> pUSD` strategy covenant, the covenant rules enforce that the received payment tokens ($pUSD$) are **not deposited into a passive pool or sent to an idle address**. Instead, the transaction atomically outputs a **brand new Reverse Strategy UTXO** (`pUSD -> BCH`) at your pre-configured buy spread.

Your capital remains perpetually in motion:
$$\text{BCH Deposit} \xrightarrow{\text{Fill}} \text{pUSD Buy Order} \xrightarrow{\text{Fill}} \text{BCH Buy Order (with Compounded Fees)}$$

### C. Sub-Cent Atomic Bulk Rebalancing
When market prices drift outside your strategy's active range, you can reconfigure your entire grid (e.g., closing 10 stale UTXOs and opening 12 new tight-spread UTXOs) in **a single transaction costing less than one penny (~$0.01 USD)**.

### D. Zero-Inventory Solver Integration
Decentralized intent solvers and relayers monitor the BCH mempool and UTXO set. When a trader submits a swap intent (or Dutch auction order), solvers atomically route the trade directly through the best-priced BCHMesh strategy covenants. Solvers need **zero inventory of their own**—the covenant introspects the trade in real time and settles atomically.

## 5. How It Works: The Lifecycle of Liquidity

### 1. `ship()` — Deploying Strategy
The LP deposits assets into a P2SH32 script and mints a 39-byte Parameter NFT encoding:
* Owner PKH (Public Key Hash)
* Curve Type (Constant Price, Concentrated Band, Oracle-Linked)
* Trading Fee in basis points (e.g., $30\text{ bps} = 0.3\%$)
* Price Numerator & Denominator
* Min/Max range bounds

### 2. `fill()` — Atomic Taker Execution
A trader or solver fills against the UTXO. CashScript covenant introspection verifies:
* The exact amount of required counter-asset is provided.
* The fee is correctly computed and preserved.
* A successor UTXO is created according to protocol rules (holding the remaining reserve or reverse pair).

### 3. `dock()` — Unconditional Instant Exit
At any second, without requiring permission from relayers or solvers, the LP can invoke the `dock` covenant branch with their private key to recover 100% of the funds and accumulated fees directly into their personal wallet.
